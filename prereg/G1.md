## Part A: the registered hypotheses and thresholds (unchanged from the kit)

### Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis |
|---|---|
| H-trap (P3, primary) | Inside the boundary (agent within the top k by action-conditioned predictability), plain training loses the agent, and a warm-up of inverse dynamics (first 10% of steps, weight 10) keeps it after being switched off |
| H-order (P1) | Plain training keeps the agent if and only if its passive (action-free) normalised error ranks within the top k among all factors |
| H-boundary (P3) | As agent process noise grows until the agent leaves the action-conditioned top k, warm-up persistence fails |
| H-deaf (P2) | With action autocorrelation φ, the step at which the action pathway aligns with the true effect (act_rel_err < 0.5) increases with φ |
| H-dose (P4) | There is a minimum warm-up fraction; descriptive |

### Sweeps, seeds, metrics

All from `src/sweeps.py`. Default steps 20000; evaluate every 100 steps.

| Sweep | Levels | Seeds | Primary metric |
|---|---|---|---|

| g1_rho | rho_a ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 0.97} × idm ∈ {none, always} | 0 to 2 | final agent_r2 vs the passive-ranking prediction |

### Thresholds (identical to `src/analysis/gates.py` THRESHOLDS)

#### g1_trap

All counts are out of 5 seeds.

| Criterion | Condition |
|---|---|
| Plain training fails | final agent_r2 < 0.2 in ≥ 4 seeds |
| Inverse dynamics fixes | IDM-always final agent_r2 ≥ 0.8 in ≥ 4 seeds |
| Trap, not objective (support) | IDM-always final pred_loss < plain final pred_loss in ≥ 80% of seeds |
| **PASS** | warm-up final agent_r2 ≥ 0.8 × IDM-always in ≥ 4 seeds, and the support criterion holds |
| **KILL** | warm-up final ≤ plain final + 0.1 in ≥ 3 seeds |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | the regime fails to show fail-and-fix |

#### Other sweeps

| Sweep | PASS condition |
|---|---|
| g1_rho | the passive-ranking prediction is correct at ≥ 5 of 6 rho levels (outcome "kept" = mean final agent_r2 ≥ 0.5) |
| g1_hist | Spearman(φ, escape step) ≥ 0.8 at each h. Censored runs count as 2 × the budget |
| g1_objective | warm-up persists (ratio ≥ 0.8) at every noise level where the agent is within the action-conditioned top k, and fails (ratio < 0.5) at levels where it is not. Report a level that does not fit as a failure of P3 |

### H-boundary restated (P3′)

**Hypothesis.** The warm-up persists iff the agent-kept solution is a strict local minimum, that is c_kept = min over dropped Y of [(ρ_a − ρ_Y)² + nerr_Y − nerr_agent] > 0. The global-optimum condition (original P3) is a special case. docs/01 anticipated this restatement ("if multistability, restate P3 as persists whenever the agent-kept fixed point is stable").

**Gate g1_phase.**
- **Per-cell outcome:**
  - "persists": mean warm-up/always ratio ≥ 0.8;
  - "decays": ratio < 0.5;
  - otherwise "ambiguous", which counts as wrong.
- **Validity:** a cell is valid only if mean IDM-always agent_r2 ≥ 0.8.
- **PASS** requires all of:
  - ≥ 7 valid cells;
  - the P3′ prediction correct in ≥ 80% of valid cells;
  - at least one predicted-decay cell decays;
  - at least one region-C cell persists.
- **Else FAIL.**
- Original-P3 accuracy on the same cells is reported alongside.
- The pred_loss column is descriptive (a quantitative check of the theory), not gating.

### Slow vs fast action pathway (mechanism of P1)

**Sweep g1_depth.** act_depth 1, idm none, rho_a ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9}, seeds 0 to 2 (12 runs). Paired with the depth-2 idm=none runs of g1_rho at the same rho_a and seeds.

**Predictions:**
- Depth 1 (fast V, full c):
  - evicted at 0.25 (c +0.233);
  - no prediction at 0.5 (|c| = 0.03 < margin 0.05);
  - kept at 0.75 (c −0.167) and at 0.9 (c −0.190).
- Depth 2 (slow V, passive c):
  - evicted at 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75;
  - no prediction at 0.9 (c = 0).

**Gate g1_depth.** Outcome "kept" = mean final agent_r2 ≥ 0.5. **PASS** iff:
- every predicted cell is correct; and
- at rho_a 0.75, depth 1 is kept while depth 2 is evicted.

### Secondary H-deaf (P2′)

In g1_hist, the **IDM-always** runs (the agent is encoded at every φ) should show the action-use escape step increasing with φ. Gate g1_hist_always: Spearman ≥ 0.8 at each h. Censored = 2 × budget.

### Dose threshold (P4′)

In g1_dose, read the eval at the end of the warm-up.

**Prediction:** the warm-up persists (final agent_r2 ≥ 0.8) iff, at that step:
- agent_r2 ≥ s*, where s* = 0.383 from the theory; and
- act_rel_err < 0.5.

**Gate g1_dose_threshold:** PASS iff this rule is right in ≥ 80% of the 30 runs. The R²-only rule is reported alongside.

### Proposed G1 decision rule (for the user to adopt or reject at the decision gate)

The docs/02 GO rule needs g1_objective to show persistence failing outside the action-conditioned top k. The theory predicts this will not happen at rho_a 0. So the original rule is predicted to withhold GO even if every theory prediction is right.

We propose: **GO if g1_trap PASS and g1_phase PASS.**

Both the original and the proposed rule will be reported. The choice is the user's.

## Amendments

**Amendment 1 (8 Nov 2026, after the referee check, before any G1 gate was evaluated).**
- The referee marked Result 3 (slow action pathway) CONDITIONAL (see `notes/theory.md`): with c_full < 0 < c_V0, the agent eventually enters after a plateau of roughly 1/v0² steps.
- At act_init 1e-3 this is far beyond the 20k budget, so every Part B prediction above is unchanged.
- Results 1, 2, 4 and 6 (C1 to C4 and C6) were verified.
- At registration, only two partial g1_trap logs had been glimpsed (plain runs, agent_r2 ≈ 0 near step 3700). No gate had been run.

**Amendment 2 (8 Nov 2026, before g1_reg ran): H-ema.**
- **Prediction:** with the EMA (stop-gradient) target, the action term drops out of the linearised encoder dynamics, which become 2d_j(d_j − d_i) (referee). For the action-set agent (d = 0), the agent-kept solution is then at best marginal. We predict that under reg = ema the warm-up does **not** persist: mean warm-up agent_r2 / mean IDM-always agent_r2 < 0.5.
- **Comparison:** under reg = cov and reg = sigreg the ratio should be ≥ 0.8.
- **Evaluation:** by hand, from the g1_reg descriptive report (per-cell mean agent_r2). No gate code was changed, to keep one code hash across the G1 batch.
- **Counts as:** a PASS of H-ema if both statements hold; FAIL otherwise.
