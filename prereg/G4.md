## G4a synthetic (`src/g4_align/align_synthetic.py --seeds 10`)

**World:** n = 8 latent dimensions, of which c = 4 are controllable; 2-D actions; Krylov depth 4. Factors are crossed: anisotropy ∈ {0, 0.5, 1.0} × predictor noise ∈ {0, 0.05, 0.2}.

**Metric:** mean cosine between the estimated map and the true map, applied to orthonormal bases of the controllable subspace and of its complement.

**PASS if all hold:**
- ours (controllable) ≥ 0.9 at predictor noise ≤ 0.05, for every anisotropy;
- |ours (uncontrollable)| ≤ 0.3 at predictor noise ≤ 0.05;
- Gromov-Wasserstein (controllable) ≤ 0.3 at anisotropy 0.

**Report:**
- the pairs-equivalence (the number of paired samples the oracle needs to match ours);
- the Gromov-Wasserstein trend with anisotropy.
